# Pricing & Packaging

## Model
Hybrid pricing:

```text
Base platform fee + included DSAR volume + overage
```

Rationale:
- Pure per-seat pricing undercounts value because workload is driven by request volume.
- Pure usage pricing creates budget uncertainty.
- Hybrid pricing aligns revenue with operational value while remaining predictable.

## Proposed plans

| Plan | Price | Includes | Overage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Starter** | €79/month | 1 workspace, 5 DSARs/month, 3 data-source checklists, evidence export | €15 per additional DSAR |
| **Growth** | €199/month | 3 workspaces, 25 DSARs/month, unlimited checklists, approvals, API | €10 per additional DSAR |
| **Agency** | €499/month | 15 client workspaces, 100 DSARs/month, white-label portal, priority support | €8 per additional DSAR |

## Annual discount
Offer 2 months free on annual payment:

- Starter annual: €790/year
- Growth annual: €1,990/year
- Agency annual: €4,990/year

## MRR targets
At €149 average monthly contract value:
- €2,000 MRR = approximately 14 customers
- €5,000 MRR = approximately 34 customers

## Pricing benchmarks
- DSARify: vendor-published comparison lists €49/month.
- SecureSlate: third-party comparison reports plans from $284/month.
- DataGrail, Transcend, MineOS, Ethyca: generally custom or sales-led pricing.
- OneTrust: enterprise-oriented, with reported minimums around $10,000/year in third-party coverage.

## Packaging rules
- Do not gate the evidence pack; it is the core differentiator.
- Gate multi-client workspaces for agencies.
- Gate integrations by plan tier.
- Charge for volume, not for number of internal users.
- Offer a 14-day trial with one sample request.
