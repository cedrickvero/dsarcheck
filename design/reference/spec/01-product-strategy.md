# Product Strategy — DSAR & Deletion-Evidence Workspace

## Positioning
**The DSAR and deletion-evidence workspace for small SaaS teams and agencies.**

The product turns an incoming GDPR data-subject request into a tracked, completed, and auditable case. It does not attempt to be a full GDPR compliance platform.

## Target customer
Primary:
- SaaS companies with EU/UK users
- 10–200 employees
- Support-, CRM-, and database-heavy operations
- No dedicated privacy-engineering team

Secondary:
- Privacy consultants and fractional DPOs
- Web/e-commerce agencies managing multiple client workspaces

## Core problem
When a person submits an access, rectification, portability, or erasure request, teams must identify data across systems, verify identity, redact third-party information, obtain approvals, respond within GDPR deadlines, and retain evidence.

Current options force a choice between:
- Spreadsheets, shared inboxes, and manual tracking
- Lightweight consent/policy tools that do not manage fulfillment deeply
- Enterprise privacy platforms that are expensive, integration-heavy, and sales-led

## Value proposition
> Close GDPR requests quickly, with human-approved actions and an audit-ready evidence pack—without becoming another system that stores all customer data.

## Differentiation
| Alternative | Their focus | Our focus |
|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheets / shared inbox | Ad hoc tracking | Structured workflow, SLA, approvals, evidence |
| iubenda / consent tools | Policies, cookies, consent | Request fulfillment and proof |
| DataGrail / Transcend | Integration-led automation | Low-risk guided workflow first |
| OvalEdge / data catalogs | Enterprise data discovery and governance | Individual request closure and evidence |
| DSARify / SecureSlate | SMB workflow | Deeper evidence, redaction, approvals, agency workflow |

## Business goal
Reach **$2,000–$5,000 MRR within 6 months**.

At an average contract value of €149/month, this requires approximately:
- 14 customers for €2,086 MRR
- 34 customers for €5,066 MRR

## Non-goals for v1
- Full GDPR compliance suite
- Autonomous deletion without human approval
- Broad enterprise data-catalog capabilities
- Legal advice or guaranteed regulatory compliance
- AI-generated final responses without human review
